## 3 new video ideas

**1. "Cat Court: Who Knocked the Cup Off the Table?"**
- **Hook (first 5 sec):** A deadpan judge voice says, "The defendant is accused of gravity experiments." This plays over a cat staring at a cup on the edge.
- **Why US viewers:** Courtroom and true-crime parody is familiar, and knocking things off tables is a very common cat habit. It's also easy to turn into a series.
- **Format:** Short (20 to 30s). Original narration over our own or licensed or permission-cleared clips, with caption graphics for the verdict.

**2. "Rating Cat Landings: Olympic Judges Edition"**
- **Hook:** "Judge 1 gives it a 9.5, but the dismount was suspicious." A freeze-frame lands on a cat mid-jump.
- **Why US viewers:** Olympic and sports commentary is a familiar joke format. Scorecards add a reason to watch to the end.
- **Format:** 45 to 60s compilation. We add original slow-mo, score graphics in HyperFrames and our own commentary. This is a transformative edit, and each clip needs a cleared source.

**3. "Things Your Cat Does at 3 AM, Explained by a Nature Documentary"**
- **Hook:** A calm David Attenborough-style voice (our own original narration, not an imitation of a real person's voice) says, "Here, in the dark hallway, the wild house cat begins its nightly sprint."
- **Why US viewers:** Zoomies at night is something almost every American cat owner knows. The nature-doc parody is familiar and shareable.
- **Format:** 60 to 90s narrated compilation with captions, as a long-form test. If it works, cut Shorts from it.
